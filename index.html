DISCORD SECURITY & GUIDELINES
Developer Policies, Voice Privacy & Compliance Rules
D4Hz operates in alignment with Discord Developer Policies and safety standards. Please review these 5 mandatory principles:
🤖 1. Official Discord Bots vs. User Tokens
Discord Terms of Service (Section 5) prohibits automating user accounts ("self-bots"). For 100% compliant and ban-free voice amplification, create an official Bot Application in the Discord Developer Portal and use Bot Mode.
🎙️ 2. Audio Privacy & Affirmative Consent
Under Discord Developer Policy and wiretapping laws, you must obtain consent from all speakers in a voice channel before capturing, repeating, or broadcasting audio. Unconsented voice relay violates Discord Community Guidelines on Harassment.
🔒 3. Zero Credential Exfiltration
Tokens are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage and sent only to your local machine (`127.0.0.1:7432`). They are never transmitted to external cloud servers. Tokens are masked in the UI to prevent shoulder-surfing.
⚡ 4. Rate Limiting & Gateway Compliance
The engine honors Discord API rate limit responses (`429`), handles `retry_after` backoff intervals, and maintains exact WebSocket heartbeat timing (Op 1 & Op 3) to prevent connection blacklisting.
🛡️ 5. Community Safety & Anti-Raiding
Using multi-account voice tools for channel disruption, ear-rape mic spam, or server raiding is strictly prohibited by Discord Community Guidelines. Keep voice audio clean and respectful.
